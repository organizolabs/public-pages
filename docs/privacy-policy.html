Organizo Privacy Policy

App: Organizo for Android (package com.organizo.android) · Last updated: 15 November 2026

In short: Organizo keeps your data on your phone. We have no servers and no analytics, and we don't show ads or sell data. If you choose to sign in with Google, backups go to your own Google Drive, and only the app can reach them. When the phone is online, the app asks Google Firebase whether a newer version is required; that request carries none of your entries or account details.

1. Who we are

Organizo is a personal finance app for Android with two modules: Expensio (expense tracking) and Ledger (money lent and borrowed). This policy explains what information the app handles and what it does with it.

2. Information the app handles

InformationWhere it livesWhy

The app does not collect your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, advertising ID or device identifiers. None of your expenses, ledger entries or account details are sent to Firebase.

3. Sign-in with Google and Google Drive

Signing in is optional. When you sign in, the app uses Google Sign-In to learn your account's email address and display name. To back up, it asks for one Google permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — lets the app see and manage only the files that the app itself creates in your Google Drive. It cannot read, change or list any other file in your Drive.

The app uses this permission only to upload, list, download and delete its own backup files. Data obtained from Google APIs is used only to provide these backup and restore features. It is not transferred to anyone else, not used for advertising, and not read by humans.

Organizo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can sign out in the app at any time. You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and you can delete the backup files from your Google Drive yourself.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. The only place your data leaves the device is your own Google Drive, and only if you sign in. The update check uses Google Firebase Remote Config, which processes the installation ID described in section 2 under Firebase's privacy terms. The app has no analytics, crash reporting or advertising libraries.

5. Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to sign in, to talk to Google Drive and to check whether an update is required. The app works fully offline without them.
  • Vibration — for haptic feedback.
  • Notifications — to tell you when an automatic backup failed while the app was closed. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.

6. Security

Data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on an unrooted phone. Traffic to Google uses HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. Your phone's screen lock and Google account security also protect your data.

7. Retention and deletion

  • On your device: data stays until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
  • In Google Drive: backup files stay until you delete them in Drive. Uninstalling the app does not delete them.
  • We hold no copy of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

8. Children

Organizo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
